How to Use CurePath

Follow these simple steps to get AI-powered health guidance

1
🔐
Create an Account or Login

Sign up with your name, age, gender and contact details. Your data is securely stored and used to personalise your experience.

2
🩺
Describe Your Symptoms

Type your symptoms in the chat box or use the voice mic button. The autocomplete feature helps you find exact symptom names quickly.

e.g. fever and chills dry cough, fatigue headache and nausea
3
⚡
Get an Instant Prediction

Our machine learning model analyses your symptoms and instantly predicts the most probable disease with a confidence score.

4
📊
Review Detailed Results

See the predicted disease, confidence bar, disease description, other possible conditions, and all supporting health information in one view.

5
💊
Follow Health Recommendations

Explore personalised suggestions: precautions to follow, medications, recommended diet plans, and workout routines for your condition.

6
📁
Track Your History

Every search is saved to your account. Review past predictions from the sidebar to monitor your health patterns over time.

7
👨‍⚕️
Consult a Doctor

Visit the Consultancy section to view specialist doctor profiles and know when to seek professional medical advice for your condition.

!
⚠️
Important Disclaimer

CurePath provides AI-based health guidance only. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified doctor for serious health concerns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CurePath a replacement for a doctor?
No. CurePath is an AI-powered decision-support tool. It helps you understand possible conditions based on symptoms, but a qualified medical professional should always make final diagnoses and treatment decisions.
How accurate are the predictions?
The model is trained on a curated medical symptom-disease dataset and achieves high accuracy. Each prediction comes with a confidence score. However, accuracy depends on how precisely you describe your symptoms.
Is my health data private?
Yes. Your data is stored locally in a secure SQLite database and is only accessible to your account. We do not share personal health data with third parties.
Can I use voice input?
Yes! Click the microphone icon in the chat box to speak your symptoms. Voice input works best in Chrome or Edge browsers. The system will transcribe your speech and analyse it automatically.
