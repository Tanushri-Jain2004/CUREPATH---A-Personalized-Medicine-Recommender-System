About CurePath

Smart AI-powered healthcare assistant for early disease prediction and personalised care

CurePath is an intelligent healthcare web application designed to assist users in identifying potential diseases based on their symptoms. Using machine learning algorithms, the system provides accurate predictions along with personalised recommendations including precautions, diet plans, and workout routines.

Our goal is to make healthcare more accessible, faster, and smarter by integrating Artificial Intelligence into everyday health monitoring — putting the power of early detection in everyone's hands.

What we offer
🤖

AI Prediction

Predict diseases instantly based on selected symptoms using trained machine learning models with confidence scores.

📊

Personalised Advice

Get customised precautions, diets, and workouts tailored specifically to your predicted condition.

📁

History Tracking

Store and review your previous predictions for better long-term health monitoring and trend awareness.

🎙️

Voice Assistant

Use the built-in voice assistant to describe symptoms hands-free and receive spoken health guidance.

💊

Medication Info

Receive medication suggestions and dietary recommendations specific to each predicted disease.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor Profiles

Browse specialist doctor profiles and know when to escalate to professional medical care.

⚙️ How It Works

1
Describe your symptoms in the chat box or use voice input
2
Our AI model analyses the symptom data in real time
3
The system predicts the most probable disease with a confidence score
4
Receive detailed recommendations — precautions, medications, diet & workout
5
Review your history anytime to track health patterns over time

To revolutionise healthcare by making AI-driven diagnosis accessible to everyone — ensuring early detection, better treatment outcomes, and a healthier world for all.
